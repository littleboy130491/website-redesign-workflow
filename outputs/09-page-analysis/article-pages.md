## Common Strengths Across Article Pages

1. **Consistent Structure** - All articles use same Elementor template, ensuring consistency
2. **Good Accessibility** - All images have alt text, aria attributes present (23-24 per page)
3. **Proper Heading Hierarchy** - 1 H1, 3 H2 tags per article - consistent and proper
4. **Forms Present** - 3 forms per article (likely comment, search, newsletter)
5. **Adequate Navigation** - 32-34 links per article for related content

## Common Weaknesses

1. **Very Similar Layout** - All articles appear to use identical template
2. **Minimal Images** - Only 1 image per article - content is text-heavy
3. **Stale Content** - Last modified dates from 2021
4. **No Social Sharing** - No share buttons visible in structure
5. **No Author Bio** - No author attribution on articles
6. **Outdated jQuery** - Same jQuery 1.12.4 dependency issue

## Common Gaps

1. **No Related Articles** - No suggested/related content
2. **No Categories/Tags** - No taxonomy visible
3. **No Call-to-Action** - No prompt to contact firm
4. **No Estimated Read Time** - Long articles without read time indicator
